Answers to common questions about CharaCall. If you need more help, contact us at the bottom of this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

On first launch, a guided setup walks you through five steps: create a character, allow notifications, allow calendar access, choose calendars, and send a test notification. You can try the included sample character first. To run the setup again, open Settings and choose the setup option.

How do I create a character?

Choose an image from Photos or Files and a voice file, give the character a name, and save. The voice file can be any length.

Which file types are supported?

Images: common formats such as JPEG, PNG and HEIC. Audio: common formats such as mp3, m4a and wav, of any length. If a file cannot be imported, try converting it to one of these formats.

How do I add Google Calendar?

CharaCall reads the Calendar app on your device. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings > Calendar > Accounts > Add Account > Google, sign in, and turn on Calendars. Then open CharaCall and choose the calendar. Exchange and other accounts added to the Calendar app work the same way.

How do I change which calendars, characters or timing are used?

Open CharaCall and go to Settings. There you can pick which calendars are announced, assign a character to each calendar (plus a default character), and choose to be notified at the start time and/or 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes before. All-day events notify at a time you choose.

I changed an event, but the notification did not change.

Notifications are scheduled for roughly the next 14 days and refreshed when you open CharaCall (iOS may also refresh them occasionally in the background). After you add, move or delete events, open CharaCall once. iOS also limits how many notifications an app can schedule.

Why don't I hear the character's voice?

Check the following:

  • The ringer is on. The Silent switch (or ringer setting) mutes notification sounds, and the "Break Through Focus" setting cannot override it.
  • The volume is turned up.
  • A Focus may be silencing notifications. Turn on "Break Through Focus" in CharaCall Settings to let them arrive during a Focus, and check that the Focus allows time-sensitive notifications.
  • Notifications are allowed for CharaCall in the device Settings, with sounds turned on.
  • For notifications, only the first 25 seconds of the voice file play. The full voice plays when the app is open.

Is this an alarm?

No. CharaCall is a notifier. It does not repeat until you dismiss it. For wake-up alarms, please use your iPhone's built-in alarm feature.

How does the stopwatch announcement work?

Open the Stopwatch, choose how often the character should announce (every 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes), and start. Announcements are delivered as notifications, so they work even when the app is closed.

How do I remove a character?

Open the character list, then delete the character. Its imported image and voice are removed from the app's storage on your device. Deleting CharaCall removes everything.

Can I use it on a Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon, CharaCall is available as an iPad app (Designed for iPad). It reads the calendars of the Calendar app on your Mac, including iCloud calendars. Because of a macOS restriction, imported files cannot be used as notification sounds on a Mac: notifications use the standard sound, and the character's voice plays while the app is open.

Is my data collected?

No. CharaCall has no network access and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

What about rights to images and audio?

Use only images and audio you own or have permission to use. CharaCall plays only files that you import yourself.
