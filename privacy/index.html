Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22

Summary

CharaCall collects no data. It has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Everything stays on your device.

Calendar

With your permission, CharaCall reads events from the Calendar app on your device, only to schedule local notifications. Events are processed on the device and are never sent anywhere. If you have added an account such as Google or iCloud in iPhone Settings, syncing of that account is handled by iOS and that provider, not by CharaCall.

Images and audio you import

Images and audio files you choose are copied into the app's private storage on your device so that characters can work. They are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time by deleting the character, or by deleting the app. CharaCall accesses Photos or Files only when you choose an item.

Notifications and settings

Notifications are generated locally on your device. Your settings are stored only on your device.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, store on any server, sell or share your data. No third parties receive any information from the app.

Children's privacy

CharaCall does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
